Scale marker

  1. Print this page at 100% (no "fit to page"). The black square should be about 15 cm wide.
  2. Measure the black square edge to edge with a ruler and enter that size (cm) in RoomSketch → Options.
  3. Lay it flat on the floor or tape it to a wall where several photos will see it.

ArUco DICT_4X4_50, id 0. Any marker from that dictionary works; use several for big rooms.
